Privacy Policy

FormShield - Auto Save Forms

Last Updated: December 14, 2025

🔒 SUMMARY: This extension stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Data Storage

  • All form data is saved in your browser's local storage
  • Data never leaves your computer
  • No external servers are used
  • No analytics or tracking
  • Triple redundancy: Chrome Storage, IndexedDB, and localStorage

Data We Never Save

  • Passwords
  • Credit card numbers
  • Social security numbers (SSN)
  • CVV/CVC codes
  • PIN numbers
  • Data from banking websites (Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, PayPal, Venmo)
  • Data from healthcare portals
  • Any fields marked as sensitive

Your Control

  • View saved data anytime in the extension popup
  • Delete all saved data with one click
  • Data automatically expires after 30 days
  • Manually save or restore data at any time

Permissions Explained

"Read and change all your data on websites" - This permission is required to detect form fields and save your input. We only save form data locally; we cannot read your passwords or browsing history.

"Storage" - Required to save form data locally in your browser's secure storage.
